My Work

Deserted - Zombie Survival

Deserted - Zombie Survival game is an adventure, open-world, zombie-survival game that features crafting, base building, collecting resources, hunger and thirst, weather, weapons, cars, and more. You will first customize your character to fit what you want. Choose between different occupations to survive the zombie apocalypse.
This game was published on Google Play and has reached over 600,000 total downloads.

Shiba Jumpu

Shiba Jumpu is a casual mobile endless runner game where you control a shiba inu. The goal is to jump over platforms and reach as far as possible without falling. The game features simple controls which makes it easy to pick up and play.

Elude: Infinity

Elude: Infinity is a casual mobile endless runner game where you control a triangle character. There are a lot of obstacles that you need to avoid to keep on increasing your score. Collect coins to unlock new characters and abilities. The game features simple controls which makes it easy to pick up and play.

[Prototype] Basketball game

[Prototype] Basketball game is a prototype basketball game where you can shoot and score points. This game is a prototype to test mechanics of shooting and game feels.

Tecnam P2006T - Aviation VR Training

Tecnam P2006T VR Training (Aviation Students) is a VR simulation designed for aviation students. This helps aviation students to practice and familiarize with the Tecnam P2006T aircraft in a virtual environment. The simulation aims to enhance learning and provide a safe training experience and allow human errors without physical risks. I worked on the development and implementation of the VR simulation. Art provided by client.

Ground Ops - Aviation VR Training
